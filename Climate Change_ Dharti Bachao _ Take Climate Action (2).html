FAQs

Questions, answered

What is the difference between weather and climate?

Weather is short-term conditions; climate is the long-term pattern, typically averaged over 30 years or more.

How do greenhouse gases warm Earth?

They absorb heat radiated by Earth and re-emit part of it downward, so the lower atmosphere stays warmer.

Can one person really make a difference?

Individual actions add up and influence communities and policy, but large cuts also need clean energy, efficient industry and good policy.

Is rooftop solar worth it?

It can reduce grid electricity use and emissions. Savings depend on roof, usage, tariff and scheme rules, so compare written quotes from registered vendors and check official portals for current subsidy.

Is the data here live?

No. Figures are labelled approximate snapshots from IPCC, NOAA and CEA. See their sites for the latest.
